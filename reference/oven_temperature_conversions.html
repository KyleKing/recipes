Common Temperatures

Listed as Fahrenheit, conventional Celsius, fan Celsius, and gas mark.

  • 275 °F / 140 °C / 120 °C fan / gas 1 (cool)
  • 300 °F / 150 °C / 130 °C fan / gas 2 (cool)
  • 325 °F / 160 °C / 140 °C fan / gas 3 (moderate)
  • 350 °F / 180 °C / 160 °C fan / gas 4 (moderate)
  • 375 °F / 190 °C / 170 °C fan / gas 5 (moderately hot)
  • 400 °F / 200 °C / 180 °C fan / gas 6 (moderately hot)
  • 425 °F / 220 °C / 200 °C fan / gas 7 (hot)
  • 450 °F / 230 °C / 210 °C fan / gas 8 (hot)
  • 475 °F / 240 °C / 220 °C fan / gas 9 (very hot)

Formulas

  • °C = (°F − 32) × 5 / 9
  • °F = (°C × 9 / 5) + 32

Notes

  • A fan (convection) oven runs about 20 °C hotter than the dial suggests, so drop the conventional Celsius figure by 20 °C or the Fahrenheit figure by 25 °F
  • Oven thermostats drift, so an oven thermometer is worth more than any conversion chart
