Blends

1 Tbsp Italian Seasoning

  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp dried rosemary

1 Tbsp Herbes de Provence

  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried savory or oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1/2 tsp dried marjoram
  • 1/4 tsp dried lavender

1 Tbsp Poultry Seasoning

  • 1.5 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/4 tsp dried marjoram
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

1 Tbsp Chili Powder

  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • Cayenne to taste

1 Tbsp Taco Seasoning

  • 1.5 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt

1 Tbsp Pumpkin Pie Spice

  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp ground allspice or cloves

1 Tbsp Apple Pie Spice

  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom

1 Tbsp Cajun Seasoning

  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

1 Tbsp Curry Powder

  • 1.5 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • Cayenne to taste

Garam Masala

See Spices: Garam Masala

Notes

  • Ground spices fade after about a year, so a blend mixed from an old jar will taste flat no matter the ratio
  • Blends built on chilies (chili powder, Cajun, curry) vary in heat by brand, so mix without the cayenne and add it at the end
