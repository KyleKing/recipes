Ingredient Substitutions

Flour and buttermilk ratios from King Arthur on self-rising flour (wayback)

Personal rating: Not yet rated

No image yet

Dairy

1 cup Buttermilk

  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar

Stir and let it sit 10 minutes until it thickens and curdles. Plain yogurt or sour cream thinned with milk works too.

1 cup Heavy Cream, for cooking

  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 1/4 cup melted butter

This will not whip. Nothing substitutes for cream in whipped cream.

1 cup Sour Cream

  • 1 cup plain full-fat Greek yogurt

Baking

1 cup Self-Rising Flour

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt

1 cup Cake Flour

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour with 2 Tbsp removed
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch

Sift together twice.

1 cup Brown Sugar

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 Tbsp molasses

Use 2 Tbsp molasses for dark brown sugar.

1 Egg, in baking

  • 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed with 3 Tbsp water, rested 5 minutes
  • Or 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce

Neither works where the egg is doing the structural work, as in a custard or a meringue.

Pantry

1 Tbsp Cornstarch, for thickening

  • 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour

1 cup Tomato Sauce

  • 1/2 cup tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup water

1 Tbsp Fresh Herbs

  • 1 tsp dried herbs

Dried herbs want simmering time, so add them early and add fresh ones at the end.
