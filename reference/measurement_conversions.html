Volume

  • 1 tsp = 5 ml
  • 1 Tbsp = 3 tsp = 15 ml
  • 1/4 cup = 4 Tbsp = 60 ml
  • 1/3 cup = 5 Tbsp + 1 tsp = 80 ml
  • 1/2 cup = 8 Tbsp = 120 ml
  • 1 cup = 16 Tbsp = 8 fl oz = 237 ml
  • 1 pint = 2 cups = 473 ml
  • 1 quart = 2 pints = 4 cups = 946 ml
  • 1 gallon = 4 quarts = 16 cups = 3.79 L

Weight

  • 1 oz = 28 g
  • 1 lb = 16 oz = 454 g
  • 1 stick butter = 8 Tbsp = 1/2 cup = 113 g

Cup Weights

Volume measures of dry ingredients vary with how the cup is packed, so weigh when the ratio matters.

  • All-purpose flour: 120 g
  • Bread flour: 120 g
  • Granulated sugar: 198 g
  • Brown sugar, packed: 213 g
  • Confectioners’ sugar, unsifted: 113 g
  • Cocoa powder, unsweetened: 84 g
  • Rolled oats: 113 g
  • Butter: 226 g
  • Honey: 336 g
  • Water or milk: 227 g

Notes

  • Fluid ounces measure volume and ounces measure weight, and they only coincide for water
  • A US cup is 237 ml; recipes written outside the US often mean a 250 ml metric cup
