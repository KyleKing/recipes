Kung Pao Chicken

Based on The Woks of Life

Personal rating: Not yet rated

No image yet

Ingredients

Peanuts

  • 1 tsp vegetable oil, for peanuts
  • 1 cup raw shelled peanuts (with or without the skin)

Chicken and Marinade

  • 12 oz chicken breast, cut into 3/4-inch cubes (or boneless skinless chicken thighs)
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil, for the marinade
  • 1 tsp cornstarch, for the marinade
  • 1 tsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1 pinch white pepper

Sauce

  • 1 Tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp rice wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 Tbsps water
  • 1 tsp cornstarch, for the sauce

Stir-Fry

  • 3 Tbsps oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed and sliced
  • 2 thin slices ginger, minced
  • 2 dried red chilies, deseeded and chopped
  • 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorn powder
  • 6 scallions, white portions only, cut into 3/4-inch pieces

Recipe

  1. Heat 1 tsp oil in a wok or pan over medium heat and add 1 cup raw shelled peanuts. Stir constantly (or they’ll burn) for 3 minutes. Turn off the heat and stir for another minute using the residual heat in the wok. Set aside to cool completely so they turn crunchy.
  2. Combine the chicken with 1 tsp each of oil, cornstarch, and Shaoxing wine, 1/8 tsp salt, and a pinch of white pepper. Set aside to marinate for 20 minutes.
  3. In a small bowl, mix together light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, sugar, water, and cornstarch. Set aside.
  4. Heat 2 Tbsps oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Sear the chicken, remove from the wok to a bowl, and set aside.
  5. Turn the heat to low and add another Tbsp oil. Add the garlic, ginger, chilies, Sichuan peppercorn powder, and scallions. Cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Add the chicken back to the pan and turn the heat to high. Stir-fry for 1 minute, then stir up the prepared sauce so the settled cornstarch is incorporated. Add the sauce to the wok and stir-fry for another minute; the sauce should thicken very quickly.
  7. Add the peanuts, give everything a final stir, and serve.

Notes

  • Substitute roasted shelled peanuts and skip the first step.
  • Boneless skinless chicken thighs can replace the chicken breast.
  • Dried red chilies can be quite spicy; adjust to taste.
