Hummus

Based on this recipe from the Washington Post (wayback). Another alternative is this recipe from the NYT (wayback)

Personal rating: 5 / 5

Ingredients

  • Process (1 Minute)
    • 3/4 cup tahini (shake to mix)
    • 1/2 cup water
    • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
    • 1 tsp cumin
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 2 garlic cloves, diced
    • 1 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    • (Optional) 2-3 Tbsp Harissa, additional for topping
    • Optional Additional Seasonings: Coriander, Caraway, Cayenne Pepper, Smoked Paprika, Chopped Mint, etc.
  • Process (3 Minutes)
    • 2 can chickpea, rinsed and drained
    • (Optional) up to 1/4 cup of water if too thick
  • Optional Toppings
    • Extra Virgin Olive Oil
    • Harissa
    • Parsley
    • Za’atar
    • Roasted Red Peppers

Recipe

  1. (Optional) to roast red peppers, remove core and cut peppers into large flat pieces. Place skin-side up on baking sheet and broil or air fry at 400°F for 5–10 minutes until skin chars. Transfer to sealed bag or covered bowl for 10–15 minutes to steam, then peel away charred skin before cutting into smaller pieces to use as a topping
  2. In a food process, combine the first set of ingredients until combined, then add the chickpeas and process until smooth
  3. Serve at room temperature. Can serve in a shallow dish and make a small well for the optional toppings
