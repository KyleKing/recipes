Basic Waffles

Based on appalachiancastiron.com

Personal rating: Not yet rated

No image yet

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1-2 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) vegetable oil or 1 stick (113 g) melted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1.75 cups (420 ml) milk (up to 2 cups for desired consistency)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional, but recommended)
  • Non-stick cooking spray

Recipe

  1. Preheat the waffle iron to about 350F, rotating it every minute or so while heating for even temperature.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, sugar, and cornstarch until uniformly blended.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk the oil (or melted butter) and eggs vigorously until fully emulsified.
  4. Whisk in the milk and vanilla until smooth. If the batter seems too thick, whisk in up to 1/4 cup additional milk to get a batter that flows easily.
  5. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and whisk gently until just combined; small lumps are okay. Avoid over-mixing to keep waffles tender.
  6. Spray both plates of the iron lightly with non-stick cooking spray.
  7. Ladle about 3/4 cup batter into the center of the iron, spreading it toward the edges so it fills to about an inch from the perimeter if needed.
  8. Close the lid and rotate the iron every minute. When steam slows to a trickle (typically 3-5 minutes), lift the lid to check for even browning.
  9. Remove waffles with a fork and transfer to a wire rack, or place single-layered on a baking sheet in a warm oven (200F) to keep crisp.

Notes

  • Sugar promotes browning: start with 1 Tbsp and add a second for darker, sweeter waffles
  • Cornstarch is a game-changer for extra-crisp exteriors
  • Keep waffles single-layered to prevent steam-softening
  • Fold in mini chocolate chips, cooked sweet potato and ginger, dried fruit, or nuts for a twist
  • Melted butter enriches flavor but may brown faster, so watch cooking times closely
